02What the service is

Quasor is a CRM for real estate agencies that automates lead capture, management, and follow-up. Specifically, the service includes:

  • Automatic lead ingestion from Meta Lead Ads, Google Ads, WhatsApp Business, and other authorized integrations.
  • Assignment of leads to sales reps in the agency, with automatic email notification to the assigned rep.
  • Property catalog synchronization through integration with Tokobroker, to link each lead with the property they inquired about.
  • Schedules of visits and meetings, and receipts and payment records associated with the operation.
  • Analytics dashboards and conversion metrics on commercial activity.

The service is delivered under a SaaS (Software as a Service) model. Commercial conditions (scope, monthly fee, included modules, implementation timelines) are agreed case by case in a discovery meeting and are reflected in a commercial proposal signed before onboarding.
